# Participant script

What I say and do with each person, so every session runs the same way.

## Before starting
> "Thanks for helping me! I'm testing a small 3D game for a research project about how people judge distance on a screen. It takes about 5 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers. I'm testing the game, not you. Your results will only be used for my learning and my portfolio, and I won't share your name publicly. Is that okay?"

Only continue if they say yes.

## Setting up
1. Open **https://mmeso-iconic.github.io/depth-guides** on their phone.
2. Ask them to hold the phone **sideways**.
3. Let them read the welcome screen. Don't explain the guides in advance.

## During the game
- Answer questions about the controls only ("Which button moves it away?").
- Don't give hints about where the target is.
- Note anything interesting they say or do, for example "Oh, I thought it was closer!" These comments are useful data.

## After the game
1. Ask them to **screenshot the results screen** and send it to me.
2. Ask one open question: *"What made it hard to tell where the ball was?"* Write down their answer.
3. Thank them.
